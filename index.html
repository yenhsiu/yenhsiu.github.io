Residual Vector Quantization-Based Dynamic Bottleneck for Latency-Accuracy Trade-off in Split Inference
Chen Yen-Hsiu, et al.
IEEE Internet of Things Journal — under review
Paper link will be added once the review process concludes.
Extended journal version of the GlobeCom 2024 paper below. Introduces RQDB, a hierarchical residual VQ module inserted at a fixed split point and trained progressively so each quantization level is independently task-optimized, letting the compression rate adapt to runtime bandwidth without retraining or repartitioning. On EfficientNet-B0/B4 image classification, RQDB holds a 90 ms real-time target while reaching up to 4.86× speedup over Cloud-INT8; on PointPillars 3D detection, it retains 95.2% of FP32 accuracy at 34.13× data reduction and 90.9% at 85.33× reduction.
Enhancing Latency-Accuracy Tradeoff in Dynamic Split Inference via Vector Quantized Bottleneck
Chen Yen-Hsiu, Yoichi Hirose, Shoma Shimizu, Shota Saito, Kento Uchida, Shinichi Shirakawa, Takayuki Nishio
2024 IEEE GlobeCom Workshops — Ubiquitous Network Intelligence for Next Generation Wireless Networks
Cape Town, South Africa, December 8–12, 2024
Paper
Introduces a vector-quantized bottleneck embedded at multiple candidate split points in the model, compressing split-inference traffic by 50–200× depending on split point and enabling dynamic split-point selection to fit the available communication bandwidth. On EfficientNet-B0, this cuts total latency by roughly 5–90% across communication speeds with only minimal accuracy loss.
